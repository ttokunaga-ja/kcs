# Quality Walkdown Follow-up



## Closeout Method

An action is closed only when the responsible owner has supplied the evidence and the reviewer has recorded the confirmation date.

Items that cannot be closed before the next huddle carry a new owner and date. This note is not a substitute for the project register.

- Review evidence at the next huddle
- Archive superseded attachments
- Notify the quality lead of any delay

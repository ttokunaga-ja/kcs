## Field Survey Witness Procedure

This note is used by the Aoba Station Front Complex quality team before a setting-out witness visit in the Level 4 north-east work area.

The witness confirms that the current construction drawing, the shared coordinate setting, and the instrument calibration record are available at the work face.

- Agree the observation boundary with the foreman
- Record the reference points in the field book

## Recording and Closeout

The field book transcription, witness photographs, and any follow-up note should be registered on the same day.

Where an item needs rechecking, assign an owner and review date at the quality huddle.

- Upload evidence to CDE
- Notify the site manager
- Close only after the recheck is recorded

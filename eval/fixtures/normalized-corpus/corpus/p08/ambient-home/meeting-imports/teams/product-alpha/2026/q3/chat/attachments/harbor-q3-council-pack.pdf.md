# Harbor Q3 council pack

Northline Labs | 24 July 2026 | 45 minute working session

Purpose

Align on the smallest discovery package that can move from customer evidence into a buildable Q4 beta shape.

Decision owners

Product, Search Platform, Research Operations, and Customer Enablement.

Pre-read signal

Account teams need clearer evidence trails when explaining why a result was surfaced or held back.

WHAT WE NEED FROM THE ROOM

**Choose the learning boundary.** Keep the Q3 prototype focused on search-intent capture, reviewer comparison, and a traceable handoff to account teams. The council is not being asked to approve a broad workflow redesign.

Agenda

- Review three recurring moments of friction from moderated sessions.
- Agree the evidence a reviewer must see before sharing a recommendation.
- Confirm which platform dependencies need an owner before the Q4 beta planning checkpoint.

Working materials

- Intent pattern brief and latest interview synthesis
- Prototype walkthrough board
- Dependency confidence snapshot

PROPOSED OUTCOME

**Council note:** move forward with a narrow learning loop that connects a saved search, reviewer rationale, and account context. Product will publish the decision log after the session; platform and research owners will add assumptions directly to the shared workspace.

Prepared for the Harbor product council. Working copy for discussion; numbers and sequencing remain subject to team review.

# HARBOR / PROTOTYPE WALKTHROUGH

Discovery review board - Q3 field session

WORKING BOARD



## A shared view of how a customer cue becomes a reviewable story

The walkthrough keeps context, evidence, and a short rationale in one continuous path.



### 1. CUE



#### Account signal

A team prepares for a renewal conversation and wants the latest context without rebuilding the story.



#### STARTING CONTEXT

**Customer thread • active question recent implementation notes**



### 2. REVIEW



#### Review surface

A reviewer compares a small group of connected sources before deciding what is safe to carry forward.



#### EVIDENCE TRAIL

Account context is visible

Source freshness is readable

Difference can be explained



### 3. HANDOFF



#### Handoff note

The next owner sees the source trail and one sentence of reasoning - not a long reconstruction of the meeting.



#### SHARED RATIONALE

“This account pattern is worth following because the support signal and implementation note point in the same direction.”



#### WALKTHROUGH FOCUS

**Can a person move from a customer cue to evidence and a reusable note without losing the thread?**

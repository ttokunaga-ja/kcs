# Discovery reduces uncertainty before beta

A small research loop keeps the team close to what people need next.

|  Question | What we will learn | Owner | Next signal  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  **Capture to recall** | Whether a short record helps people recover the thread. | Research | Interview playback  |
|  **Reflection prompt** | Which prompt makes a next question feel useful. | Product | Prototype session  |
|  **Beta boundary** | What remains out of scope for the first learning loop. | Design | Scope review  |

The next checkpoint should bring the research signal back to the product team intact.

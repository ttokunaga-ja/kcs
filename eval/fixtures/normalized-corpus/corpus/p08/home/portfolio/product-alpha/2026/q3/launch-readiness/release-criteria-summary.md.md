# Early-access release criteria



## Purpose

This summary defines the conditions for inviting the first Harbor teams. It is intentionally narrower than a general-availability checklist.



## Criteria

| Area | Ready when | Evidence expected |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First-use experience | A new reviewer can create or review one signal without facilitation | Observed walkthrough and support review |
| Ownership | A signal can be assigned, held pending, or handed off with a reason | Scenario checks across roles |
| Evidence | Shared context shows a confirmation date and access boundary | Research Ops sign-off |
| Support | Customer Success has an escalation path and a known response window | Coverage note and dry run |
| Measurement | The weekly readout can distinguish activity from completed handoffs | Analytics review |



## Out of scope

Recommendation quality, broad migration tooling, and automated summaries are not launch gates. They may be explored later, but they should not obscure whether the basic workflow is trustworthy.



## Decision rule

If a criterion is unclear, the owner records the uncertainty and proposes a limited observation plan. We do not convert an unknown into a green check merely to preserve momentum.

# Experiment guardrails for Harbor activation



## What we are learning

We are testing whether a clearer ownership-and-evidence flow helps teams continue a customer handoff without re-reading every prior conversation.



## Guardrails

- Do not alter access boundaries for the sake of a faster test.
- Keep a manual path whenever a suggested owner is uncertain.
- Separate observed behavior from customer quotes in every readout.
- Avoid comparing teams with materially different onboarding support as if they were equivalent.
- Stop an experiment when it creates ambiguity about who may view an evidence item.



## Interpretation

A higher activity count is not automatically better. The useful outcome is a handoff that remains legible to another reviewer, with an explainable reason for any pending state.



## Review cadence

Product, Research Ops, and Customer Success review the evidence weekly. Changes are small, reversible, and documented with the question they were meant to answer.

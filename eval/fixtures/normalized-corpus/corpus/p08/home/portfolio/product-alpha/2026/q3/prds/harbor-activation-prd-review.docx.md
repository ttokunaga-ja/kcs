# Activation entry-flow product review

Harbor | Northline Labs | Q3 2026



## Review outcomes

The group accepted the entry-flow direction and asked for a lighter setup sequence before implementation begins.



## Review purpose

This review aligns the Harbor activation entry flow with the evidence gathered from B2B search and discovery research. The product intent is to help account teams reach a useful discovery moment without needing an operator to translate the workspace.



### Accepted direction

Design will keep the account context visible through the first saved list. Product and data will use the same event names in the release review packet.



### Open dependencies

Support needs a short explanation of account roles, and platform needs a stable account-context contract. The Q4 Beacon beta companion should consume the same model but is not a release dependency.



### Product intent

The first-use experience should establish account context, make the result set understandable, and show the next action in one continuous interaction. The release does not attempt to solve every research workflow.



### Research follow-through

The team will revisit the flow after the first cohort and compare interview evidence with saved-list behavior before expanding the activation surface.

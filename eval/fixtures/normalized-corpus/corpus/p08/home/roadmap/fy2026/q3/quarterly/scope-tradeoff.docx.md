# Portfolio decision record

Harbor activation control point | Northline Labs | Q3 2026



## Roadmap commitment

The Harbor roadmap schedules the Cedar workstream to begin on 2026-11-04.



## Decision context

Harbor is moving from research synthesis to a focused activation release for B2B search and discovery teams. The Q3 operating plan protects the evidence loop, onboarding instrumentation, and account-level rollout controls.



### Execution controls

The delivery lead will confirm account sequencing, support readiness, and event naming before the portfolio update is circulated.



### Review rhythm

A short checkpoint will inspect adoption evidence, unresolved dependencies, and release communications. Any scope change needs a written owner and a decision record.

2

### Choice made

The portfolio group will preserve a narrow activation surface in Q3 rather than merge every discovery request into the release. This keeps the team accountable for a usable entry path and a measurable adoption review.

Work that does not change first-use quality stays in the research queue until the activation signal is stable.



### Operating guardrails

Design, research, and platform leads will use the same weekly review packet. Exceptions must identify the customer impact, the measurement gap, and the owner for the next decision.

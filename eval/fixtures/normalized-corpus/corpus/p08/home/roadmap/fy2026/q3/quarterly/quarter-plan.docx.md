# Quarterly operating outline

Harbor activation planning packet | Northline Labs | Q3 2026



## Planning cadence

The Harbor roadmap schedules the Cedar workstream to begin on 2026-11-11.



## Planning assumptions

The activation team will keep the release surface narrow while research partners validate account setup and result quality. The focus is an auditable first-use flow, not a broad feature bundle.



### Control points

Before the internal portfolio note is released, owners will verify readiness, data quality, and the intended rollout cohort.



### Decision log

Material tradeoffs will be recorded with a named decision maker and the evidence used.

2

### Work allocation

Product, design, and data will maintain a shared weekly check-in. Each item needs an explicit owner, acceptance signal, and customer communication route.



### Capacity note

The plan reserves time for implementation handoffs, support preparation, and instrumentation review. Optional experiments remain in the discovery backlog until evidence supports promotion.

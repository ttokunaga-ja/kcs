## Keller-Roth correspondence: donor overview

A working note for the *Afterlives of Judgment* research file

**Purpose.** This note records the context supplied with the M. Keller family deposit and the practical limits agreed for its use in the dissertation archive. The material is useful not as a complete intellectual biography, but as a record of how reading, translation, and judgment circulated between private letters and public lectures.

|  **Deposit** | Keller-Roth correspondence, provisional accession AR-KR-1986-97  |
| --- | --- |
|  **Coverage** | Letters, marginal reading slips, and two envelopes of seminar handouts  |
|  **Research use** | Chapter two citation recovery; chapter three source comparison  |
|  **Contact** | Consolidated questions by email, with a short list of folio references  |



### What the donor emphasized

- The letters were kept in chronological groups by Keller, while Roth's later notes were inserted by theme.
- Pencil marks in the margins are part of the reading record and should be described rather than silently normalized.
- Several names in the handouts are initials only; attribution should remain provisional until the seminar registers are checked.

**Handling for the current pass.** Work from the digital surrogates for routine citation recovery. Mark uncertain readings in the shared correction sheet, and reserve requests for the original folders for cases where the order of leaves or a physical mark changes the interpretation.

Prepared for the archive review folder. Internal working copy; no publication clearance implied.

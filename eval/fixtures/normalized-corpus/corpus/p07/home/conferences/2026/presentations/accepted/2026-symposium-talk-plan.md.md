# Littoral Symposium — talk production plan

**Working title:** Afterlives of Judgment
**Format:** short research presentation with a small set of annotated folios



## Story of the talk

1. Introduce the Keller-Roth papers and the problem of reading across languages.
2. Show how folder order and marginal marks change the interpretation of one letter.
3. Close with the dissertation method: citations, translations, and provenance remain visible together.



## Production checklist

- Confirm which images may be projected under the archive's reading-room terms.
- Use a plain source line below each image and keep the slide text spare.
- Rehearse the transition from the manuscript example to the bibliography method.
- Prepare a handout that names editions carefully and leaves uncertain readings marked as such.

The presentation should feel like a research conversation, not a claim that
every archival ambiguity has been solved.

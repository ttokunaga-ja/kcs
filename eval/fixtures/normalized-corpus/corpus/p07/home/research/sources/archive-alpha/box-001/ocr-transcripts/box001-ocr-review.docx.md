# Box 001 OCR Review

Archive Alpha — Keller–Roth correspondence transcript and derivative check



## Reading and citation practice

Quotations for the dissertation should be checked against the image before they enter a chapter draft. When a sentence depends on spacing, deletion, or a marginal addition, the relevant note should name the folio and describe the feature in ordinary editorial language. The transcript is not a substitute for that review.

The correspondence is particularly sensitive to changes of hand and to shifts between German, French, and English. Keeping the source language visible in the working notes will make later translation choices easier to audit, especially where an English paraphrase was prepared only for internal discussion.



## Purpose and scope

This working review records the current condition of the OCR transcripts associated with Archive Alpha, Box 001. It is intended to support close reading of the Keller–Roth correspondence while keeping a clear distinction between the page images, the machine-generated transcript, and the editorial text prepared for the dissertation.

The review covered the available letter images, the derivative naming pattern, and the transcript fragments retained alongside the images. It did not attempt to settle authorship questions or normalize every historical spelling. Those decisions belong in the annotation record after the relevant folios have been read against the images.



## Handover

The next pass should focus on the few images whose reading order remains unclear. Once those are checked, the team can update the transcript index and move the settled items into the manuscript source table. No derivative should be treated as a final scholarly transcription until that image-level comparison is complete.

This review is a handover record rather than a publication-ready finding. It preserves the practical context needed to continue the work: what was inspected, which uncertainties remain visible, and why the image and transcript must continue to be read together.

## Observed condition

Most files can be linked to a letter sequence through the existing folder order and the visible date line. A smaller group has uneven margins, faint carbon copies, or handwritten additions near the salutation. For these pages the transcript is useful as a finding aid, but the image remains the authority for quotation and punctuation.

The current derivatives preserve enough information to trace a line back to its image, although several filenames were created during an earlier export and do not follow the newer convention. Rather than rename material in place, the review records the relationship between the old labels and the working sequence used by the research team.



## Editorial handling

Uncertain readings are marked in the transcription notes with a short explanation of the visual problem: bleed-through, cropped edge, folded paper, or overwritten character. This keeps the transcript legible without presenting a speculative expansion as if it were part of the original letter.

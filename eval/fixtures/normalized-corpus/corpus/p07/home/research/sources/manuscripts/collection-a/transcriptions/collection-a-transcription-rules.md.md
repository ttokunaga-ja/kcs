# Collection A transcription conventions

These conventions are for the Keller-Roth working drafts and are deliberately
modest: the transcript should make the manuscript legible without pretending
that every editorial choice is certain.



## House rules

- Keep original paragraph breaks when the page makes them visible.
- Put doubtful readings in square brackets with a short reason in the note field.
- Represent a deletion as a deletion; do not replace it with the presumed intended wording.
- Record inserted words at their physical location and add a brief placement note only when necessary.
- Preserve language switches. A translation belongs in the parallel working note, not inside the diplomatic transcript.



## Review sequence

First reader records the page faithfully. A second reader checks uncertain
names, dates, and heavily revised lines against the image. Once both readings
are stable, the item can be cited from the appendix register.

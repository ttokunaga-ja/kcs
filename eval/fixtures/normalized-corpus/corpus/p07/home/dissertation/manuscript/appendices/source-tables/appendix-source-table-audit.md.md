# Appendix source-table audit

The appendix register is the bridge between the archive notes and the
dissertation's short citations. It needs to be boringly dependable.



## Checks completed

- Container labels follow the reading-room form used in the current requests.
- Folio ranges are retained even when an individual leaf is still under review.
- Language and document kind are recorded separately, which makes mixed-language drafts easier to find.



## Before the next export

| Field | Check |
|---|---|
| Source reference | unique across Archive Alpha, Archive Beta, and Collection A |
| Provenance note | explains a non-obvious sequence or reading issue |
| Transcription status | distinguishes checked, in progress, and second reading requested |

Do not turn this register into an interpretive bibliography. Its job is to
make the source trail recoverable when a chapter note is revised later.

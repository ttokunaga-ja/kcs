# Annotation triage — current set

The current pass is for marginal marks in the Keller-Roth correspondence, not
for polished interpretation. Keeping those jobs separate has made the later
chapter notes much easier to audit.



## Tags in use

- `address-form` — salutation, closing, or changes in how the correspondent is named
- `editorial-mark` — underlining, caret, bracket, or insertion by a later hand
- `reading-uncertain` — a visible mark whose wording cannot yet be settled
- `provenance-note` — evidence about folder order, paper, or accompanying slip



## This session

Two pencil additions may belong to a reader rather than to either correspondent.
They stay tagged as editorial marks until a comparative read of the neighboring
leaves. The unresolved initials should not be expanded in prose merely because
a plausible name is available elsewhere in the archive.

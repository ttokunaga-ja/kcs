# Modernism context reading

This folder is a reading ledger, not a substitute for the primary archive.
The useful question is which periodical habits and editorial conventions make
the Keller-Roth marginal notes intelligible without flattening them into a
single movement or school.



## Reading priorities

- Check how multilingual reviews signaled quotation, paraphrase, and translation.
- Distinguish the date of a reprint from the date of the issue actually consulted.
- Keep notes on editorial format separate from claims about political position.



## Claims to verify before drafting

1. Whether the recurring phrase in the correspondence appears in the same form in the periodical source.
2. Which edition the later scholarship cites when it describes the seminar exchange.
3. Whether the relevant annotations are contemporary with the letter or added during later reading.

The chapter needs enough background to orient a reader, but the archive's own
material sequence remains the center of the argument.

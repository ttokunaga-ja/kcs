# Dissertation synthesis note



## Working proposition

Afterlives of Judgment asks how the Keller-Roth papers preserve intellectual
work across correspondence, marginal annotation, and translation. The
dissertation should make the archive's material order visible while explaining
why later reading copies and editions cannot be treated as neutral.



## What connects the chapters

- Chapter 1 establishes the source boundary and transcription method.
- Chapter 2 follows a small set of translation decisions through the letters and drafts.
- The appendices provide a recoverable route from short citations back to containers and folios.

# Letter set A — translation queries

**Working pair:** Keller-Roth correspondence
**Direction:** German and French materials into an English reading copy



## Terms needing a shared decision

- Keep the informal closing formula lighter than a formal letter ending.
- Where a noun can mean both judgment and assessment, retain the broader sense until the surrounding sentence is reviewed.
- Mark a phrase as unresolved when the manuscript's overwrite leaves two viable readings.



## Handoff notes

The bilingual reviewer should see the page image, diplomatic transcript, and
reading copy together. A clean English sentence is useful, but it must not
hide a deleted word, a cramped insertion, or a switch of register that matters
to the correspondence.

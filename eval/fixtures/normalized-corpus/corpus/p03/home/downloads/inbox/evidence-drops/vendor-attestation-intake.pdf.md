## Vendor Attestation Intake

Readiness note for third-party assurance materials

Prepared by Vendor Risk Desk | Review window: Q3 2026

This note records the operating intake used for supplier attestations that support Nami Grid's Tokyo service environment. It is not a vendor risk rating.



### What is handed over

The completed handoff contains the intake register row, a reference to the original material, a short coverage summary, and any documented follow-up. The handoff should explain the relationship to the service without reproducing sensitive attachments in several locations.



### Receipt and registration

The receiving analyst records the provider name, document type, coverage period, and the location of the original attachment. If a document is password protected, the password route is recorded separately from the attachment location.



### Intake objective

The intake separates three questions that are often mixed together: whether a document was received, whether its coverage is understood, and whether follow-up is required. Keeping those checks separate makes the audit package easier to review and avoids treating an attachment as an approval.

|  Checkpoint | What is checked | Recorded by  |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  Receipt | sender, version, and attached period | Vendor Risk Desk  |
|  Coverage | named service and assurance scope | service owner  |
|  Follow-up | open clarification or replacement date | vendor manager  |



### Coverage review

- Confirm that the service named in the document is the service used by Nami Grid.
- Check that the coverage period is relevant to the readiness review window.
- Identify carve-outs, customer responsibilities, or language that needs a service-owner explanation.
- Record the reviewer and the date of the coverage check in the intake register.

|  If the material is incomplete | Handling  |
| --- | --- |
|  Wrong service named | request corrected confirmation from the provider  |
|  Coverage period is stale | request current-period material or owner rationale  |
|  Carve-out is material | ask the service owner to document compensating practice  |

The intake register captures evidence status. Contractual and procurement decisions remain with the vendor manager and Legal.

### Quality checks before handoff

|  Check | Expected result  |
| --- | --- |
|  Traceability | source material and reviewer are identifiable  |
|  Period alignment | coverage period is clear to a reviewer  |
|  Open items | owners and next action are explicit  |
|  Access handling | restricted files remain in the approved location  |



### Working convention

Vendor Risk Desk sends a weekly concise status to the readiness coordinator. Urgent changes are raised outside the weekly cycle when a provider's assurance material is withdrawn or materially updated.

This is an operating note for evidence intake. It does not make a statement about a provider's overall security posture.

### Materials normally accepted

Independent assurance reports, signed security attestations, and documented responses to material control questions are accepted when the provider and period can be identified. Marketing summaries and undated slide decks can be kept as context, but do not close an evidence request on their own.

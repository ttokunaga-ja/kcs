Intake conversation flow

|  Step | Owner | Outcome  |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  01 | Hiring lead | Clarify the problem the role will solve in the first two quarters.  |
|  02 | Recruiter | Translate the problem into observable signals and a realistic market scope.  |
|  03 | Partner | Test the scope against current channels and identify likely tradeoffs.  |
|  04 | People Operations | Confirm handling of records, access, and candidate communication.  |

Calibration prompts

- Which customer workflow should feel materially better after this person has been in role?
- What evidence would distinguish hands-on operating judgment from polished presentation?
- Which adjacent backgrounds are genuinely transferable, and which are only superficially similar?
- What should a candidate know before investing time in the next conversation?

Partner handoff note

Use the shared recruiting workspace for approved updates. Do not copy interview notes into email threads or personal trackers.

Weekly quality check

At the end of each week, the recruiter and partner compare conversion signals, feedback turnaround, and any recurring reason a candidate pauses. The goal is to improve the search narrative, not to create a scorecard outside the approved process.

Escalate when

- The role scope changes enough to alter the target market.
- A candidate asks for information that is not in the approved role brief.
- A record needs to be shared beyond the named recruiting team.
- The panel identifies a capability gap that calls for a revised interview plan.

Closeout

When the search closes, retain the partner summary in the recruiting workspace and revoke temporary workspace access through the standard People Operations request. Capture lessons learned in the quarterly Atlas hiring review.

## Purpose

This guide helps a recruiting partner join a Kasa Workbench search without creating a parallel process. It is a working reference for the first intake call, not a candidate-facing brief.



## Working principle

Namioka Systems owns candidate communication, assessment records, and approval routing. Partners contribute market insight and calibrated outreach only after the role brief is confirmed.



## Before the intake call

- Confirm the current requisition owner and the weekly decision cadence.
- Read the approved role brief; use the same capability language in outreach drafts.
- Bring two market observations and one proposed sourcing sequence, not a generic talent map.
- Send attendee names one business day before the working session.



## Information exchanged during intake

|  Namioka Systems provides | Partner returns  |
| --- | --- |
|  Role context and success indicators | Search assumptions and channel rationale  |
|  Interview workflow overview | Weekly pipeline signal with blockers  |
|  Approved candidate experience guidance | Outreach drafts for review  |

# Candidate Packet Index

Assembly and access

NAMIOKA SYSTEMS
People Operations Page 2

Assembly sequence

- Confirm the requisition context is current before adding materials.
- Use the candidate's preferred name and communication details only where needed for the session.
- Add the conversation plan after the panel roles are confirmed.
- Check access before the meeting and remove temporary access when the review cycle closes.

What does not belong in the packet

Personal notes from informal conversations, duplicated interview feedback, and unrelated hiring materials should not be bundled for convenience. If an item is needed to run the conversation, the recruiter should confirm its approved location first.

Packet review

The coordinator performs a final operational check for completeness, then the panel lead confirms that the conversation plan reflects the role brief.

# Candidate Packet Index

Closeout and records

NAMIOKA SYSTEMS
People Operations Page 3

After the conversation

Reviewers enter their observations through the shared workflow. The packet itself is not a channel for decision comments; its purpose is preparation and consistent access to approved materials.

Closeout checklist

- All reviewers can locate the approved evaluation template.
- Temporary access is reviewed after the panel cycle.
- Materials are retained according to the People Operations records practice.
- Operational learnings are captured without adding personal detail to the packet index.

Owner

Recruiting Coordination maintains this index with input from People Operations and the Kasa Workbench panel lead.

## Purpose

This index shows the standard packet components used to prepare a reviewer for a Kasa Workbench conversation. It is an operational reference, not a candidate profile and not a substitute for the approved evaluation record.



## Packet components

|  Component | Use | Steward  |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  Role context | Explains the operating problem and first-quarter priorities. | Recruiter  |
|  Candidate materials | Provides the materials the candidate has chosen to share. | Coordinator  |
|  Conversation plan | Names the discussion areas and handoff points. | Panel lead  |
|  Evaluation template | Captures observations in the approved workflow. | Each reviewer  |



## Preparation standard

Reviewers should have enough context to conduct a focused conversation, while avoiding unnecessary personal information or unapproved side notes.

# Cobalt Selection Note

Atlas hiring plan | Workforce Intelligence Lead

**Prepared for** Atlas Hiring Council

**Prepared by** People Operations

**Decision window** Round-two consolidation



## Consolidated assessment

The interview group found the candidate most persuasive when translating ambiguous workforce questions into a clear operating sequence. The response style was concise, but it included enough detail for partners to understand ownership, timing, and trade-offs.

The remaining concern is calibration consistency: each evaluator should use the agreed rubric and document the reason for any material change before the council conversation. This keeps the selection record comparable with the other active Atlas searches.



## Operating conditions

The council should receive a one-page consolidation with score distribution, evidence tags, and any documented exceptions. Individual interview notes remain the source record; the consolidated view is only a coordination aid for the discussion.

No external communication should be sent while the record is incomplete. The recruiting partner will confirm reviewer availability, route the final packet, and preserve the decision trail in the standard People Operations workspace.



## Decision context

The Atlas hiring plan adds a Workforce Intelligence Lead to connect workforce signals, planning cadence, and manager-facing evidence. The current round has completed structured interviews, work-sample review, and reference readiness checks.

Cobalt is being considered against the role charter rather than against an informal composite. The group agreed that the written record should separate demonstrated scope, collaboration behavior, and conditions for a final decision.



### Next control

- Recruiting partner: confirm that every assigned reviewer has submitted a rubric-backed note.
- Hiring manager: validate the role charter against the current Atlas workforce planning assumptions.
- People Operations: prepare the council agenda once the consolidation checkpoint closes.



### Panel threshold

**The Cobalt applicant requires a panel score of 4.3 before offer review.**

The threshold is a decision guardrail, not a substitute for the written rationale. If the consolidated result does not clear it, the council should close the loop with the hiring manager and keep the requisition open for the next calibrated slate.



## Signals reviewed

- Work-sample evidence shows a practical approach to turning staffing patterns into usable operating decisions.
- Panel notes point to strong synthesis across People, Finance, and business-unit planning partners.
- Reference outreach may begin after the council records its consolidated recommendation.

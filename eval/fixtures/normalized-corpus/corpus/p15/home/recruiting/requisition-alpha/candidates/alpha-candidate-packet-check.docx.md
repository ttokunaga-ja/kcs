# Alpha Candidate Packet Check

Intake completeness for the Workforce Intelligence Lead search

**Search** FY2026 Atlas / Alpha requisition

**Record owner** Recruiting Operations

**Purpose** Packet readiness before panel routing



## Current condition

The Alpha packet workflow is functioning as intended. Most missing items were resolved during intake rather than during panel preparation, which reduced late-stage follow-up and kept the interviewer briefing focused on role evidence.

A source snapshot of 3,600 intake objects was compared with a 3,600-object reconciliation replay. The review found consistent identifiers and no unmatched packet record in the sampled workflow. The count is an operational control, not an applicant volume statement.



## Review objective

This check verifies that packets entering the Alpha interview process contain the materials needed for a consistent reviewer experience. It is a coordination review and does not make a hiring decision or replace interview feedback.

Recruiting Operations reviews source attribution, candidate contact status, consent record, scheduling context, and work-sample availability. A packet can be returned for completion without creating a negative signal about the individual concerned.



## Control point

The recruiter should complete the handoff check one business day before the scheduled panel. If a package changes after that point, the coordinator should alert the reviewer group and update the operating record rather than sending an isolated replacement attachment.

The Alpha candidate packet process is ready for the current Atlas round. Continued attention to clear ownership and minimal, purposeful documentation will support an orderly experience for candidates and interviewers alike.



## Escalation path

- Return incomplete packets to the recruiting coordinator with the missing item named plainly.
- Ask People Operations for guidance when a consent or retention question is unclear.
- Do not ask panelists to resolve documentation issues during a live interview.

A packet is considered ready when the routing check is complete and the owner can explain any intentionally unavailable item. This keeps the panel experience predictable without over-collecting material.

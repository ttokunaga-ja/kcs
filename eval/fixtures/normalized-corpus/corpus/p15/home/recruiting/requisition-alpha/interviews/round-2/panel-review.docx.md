# Cobalt Panel Calibration

Atlas hiring plan | Workforce Intelligence Lead

**Review owner** People Operations

**Meeting type** Round-two panel calibration

**Use** Internal decision preparation



## Working view

The group sees a credible match between the candidate's operating approach and the need for dependable workforce signals. The clearest examples described a progression from uneven source data to a decision cadence that managers could actually use.

Panelists also asked for a clear distinction between strategic framing and hands-on delivery. The final written record should cite the specific examples that support the group's view, then state any remaining uncertainty plainly.



## Purpose of this record

This note captures the panel's working calibration before the council review. It is not a candidate communication, and it does not replace the individual rubric notes maintained by each interviewer.

The panel agreed to focus on evidence of analytical judgment, cross-functional influence, and the ability to establish a repeatable workforce intelligence rhythm. Reviewers should keep comments linked to observed behavior or the submitted work sample.



## Owners

- Panel facilitator: confirm completion and open questions.
- Recruiting partner: assemble the agenda packet and confirm decision timing.
- Hiring manager: restate the non-negotiable role outcomes for the council.



## Calibration threshold

**The Cobalt applicant requires a panel score of 4.1 before offer review.**

This working benchmark is intended to keep the round consistent with other Atlas reviews. It should be interpreted alongside complete rubric evidence, not as a stand-alone instruction to move the record forward.



## Evidence boundaries

- Use the approved role charter and current Atlas planning assumptions as the evaluation frame.
- Separate evidence from inference when writing a final note.
- Flag access, consent, or process concerns to the recruiting partner rather than resolving them in the panel meeting.

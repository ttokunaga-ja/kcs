# Role Intake Brief

Kasa Workbench / Product Operations Analyst

NAMIOKA SYSTEMS

People Operations

Page 1

Why this role now

Kasa Workbench is entering the FY2026 Atlas hiring plan with a need for a product operations partner who can make customer signals usable across product, enablement, and support. The role is designed around operating judgment and clear follow-through rather than program administration alone.

First two-quarter outcomes

- Create a reliable view of recurring implementation friction from customer and internal signals.
- Establish a lightweight operating rhythm for issue triage across product and enablement.
- Turn agreed decisions into concise, observable follow-ups for the teams that own them.

Team context

The role sits with Product Operations and works most closely with the Kasa Workbench product lead, Customer Enablement, and People Operations for the hiring process.

Search boundaries

- Do not position the role as a general project management post.
- Avoid promising ownership of staffing, compensation, or customer contracts.
- Keep the conversation grounded in Kasa Workbench workflows, not an abstract transformation narrative.

Candidate experience

Candidates should understand the business problem, the collaborative nature of the work, and the expected interview sequence before they invest time in deeper conversations. Use the approved accommodation guidance when a candidate requests support.

Next steps

The recruiter confirms the search narrative with the hiring lead, then begins calibrated outreach. The first weekly check-in should focus on evidence quality and market signal, not volume alone.

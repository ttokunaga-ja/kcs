# Regional Planning Scenario Brief

Harborline Storefront | FY2026 Q2 refresh working note



## Owner follow-up

Sales Planning will prepare the demand narrative, Operations Planning will confirm whether service timing altered the comparison point, and Commercial Intelligence will retain the reconciliation notes with the next working version. Each owner will describe the evidence used, not simply the direction of the result.



## Why this brief was prepared

This regional brief supports the FY2026 Q2 planning refresh by summarising how the scenario view should be read in the wider commercial cadence. It is a working note for the planning group and does not replace the approved weekly-close reporting.



## Interpretation guardrails

- Do not use a regional observation as a proxy for the all-market plan.
- Do not blend scenario assumptions with published commercial results in the same visual.
- Do not revise executive-facing language until the close and source timing are reconfirmed.



## Scope and comparison rule

The review uses the current scenario run, aligned market labels, and the latest closed-week commercial record. Changes in order timing, channel classification, and late fulfilment events remain visible to the owners rather than being netted into a single adjustment.



## Decision path

The next refresh will decide whether the regional assumption remains unchanged, is adjusted with a documented driver, or is held while an operational correction is completed. This sequence keeps the planning conversation actionable without overstating a preliminary comparison.



## Regional observation

The Mosaic scenario recorded a 13.9 percent variance in the Kyushu segment. The planning group treated this as a local follow-up item, with the cause to be reviewed alongside channel mix and operating timing before the next refresh.



## Recordkeeping

The supporting workbook should retain the review date, ownership hand-offs, and the cut used for the comparison. That discipline will allow the commercial leadership group to understand why a subsequent view differs without re-running the prior session.

- Keep the scenario marker separate from the operational dashboard labels.
- Bring a market-and-channel explanation to the next commercial review.
- Confirm the weekly-close cut before any action is moved into the planning pack.

# FY2026 Q2 Planning Scenario Review

Harborline Storefront | Commercial Intelligence working review



## Decision implications

The planning team agreed to use the review as an input to the next assumption round. Sales Planning will retain the current demand posture while Operations Planning checks whether fulfilment timing explains part of the movement. Product and Finance will receive the same decision framing, with no change to the published executive tiles.



## Purpose

This review records the planning readout prepared for the FY2026 Q2 refresh. It separates scenario assumptions from the closed commercial record so the meeting can focus on how the operating plan should respond, rather than on a single headline.



## Readout method

Commercial Intelligence aligned the scenario workbook to the same market, channel, and weekly-close conventions used in the Harborline Storefront planning pack. The review retained source timing and ownership notes so that follow-up work can begin without reopening the entire model.



## Questions for the next working session

- Which weekly-close adjustments changed the comparison base, and which are expected to repeat?
- Does the channel mix require a distinct commercial action, or is the effect contained within the planning view?
- What evidence is sufficient to revise the next refresh without creating a second version of the plan?



## Review perimeter

- Use closed-week commercial records for the comparison point and keep late order adjustments in a separate reconciliation queue.
- Read market and channel together before assigning an owner; marketplace timing can move independently of direct demand.
- Treat the scenario as a planning lens, not as a replacement for the published commercial dashboard.



## Controls before reuse

Before the scenario is referenced outside the refresh meeting, the owner should confirm the reporting cut, annotate any late operational events, and preserve the calculation path used for the comparison. This keeps the narrative traceable even when the next working file is updated.



## Working conclusion

The review should remain a contained planning artifact. The next discussion will focus on driver validation, owner commitments, and the cadence for returning an updated recommendation to the commercial leadership group.

## Scenario result

The Mosaic scenario logged a 14.6 percent variance for the Kyushu segment.

The result is material enough to shape the discussion, but it is not a release gate. The working group should test the drivers beneath the movement before changing demand, service, or inventory commitments.

# EBA101 Accessibility Guide for Evidence Briefs

**Purpose** This guide helps students check whether a reader can follow the path from a claim to its supporting evidence.

**Before submission** Use headings in order, describe the learning purpose of a figure in the surrounding text, and make link text name its destination. Do not rely on color alone to distinguish revision notes.

**Reading order** A reader should encounter the claim, the source context, and the explanation of the source in the same sequence used in class.

1

# Facilitator note

Accessibility review is part of the learning design. When a student asks for a format change, first check whether the class activity itself assumes one particular way of reading, speaking, or annotating. Keep the task goal stable and offer more than one path to it.

3

## Quick self-check

- Can a reader identify the main claim without reading a visual?
- Does each figure have a nearby explanation of what it contributes?
- Are quotations introduced before they are interpreted?

**Support** Ask the instructional support queue when a format prevents you from showing your reasoning clearly.

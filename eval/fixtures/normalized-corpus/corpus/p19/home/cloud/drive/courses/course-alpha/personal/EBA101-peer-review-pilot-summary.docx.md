# Peer-review pilot summary

This pilot tested a short peer-review routine before students revised their Evidence Brief. The design goal was not to increase the number of comments, but to make each comment point back to a sentence in the draft.

Teams worked with one shared question: what evidence is doing work for the claim, and where does the writer still need to explain the link?

# What the facilitation revealed

Students could often identify a quotation, yet they needed a pause to explain why the quotation mattered to a reader. Short prompts were more useful than model answers at this stage.

The review flow also made access needs visible early, especially when the reading guide and discussion order did not match.

## Next iteration

Keep the final individual note. It gives each writer one concrete revision decision and prevents the peer exchange from ending as a general discussion.

For the next class, prepare one boundary example for facilitators and keep student support questions outside the grading reply queue.

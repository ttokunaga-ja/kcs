# Review flow and decision ownership

Study Alpha working reference



## Daily handoff

1. The CRC desk checks newly completed visits against the calendar and records any approved scheduling change.
2. The data reviewer compares required EDC fields with the site worksheet and routes incomplete items to the appropriate role.
3. Imaging Coordination confirms that a transfer acknowledgement exists before the imaging status is marked complete.
4. The study lead reviews recurring issues at the weekly operations huddle and assigns an owner with a due date.



## Before the review meeting

- Confirm that each scheduled visit has one current status in the visit calendar.
- Reconcile outstanding clarifications with the owner named in the review log.
- Confirm that image transfers have a receipt record separate from clinical source documentation.
- Check that export folders contain only de-identified working files and the approved data dictionary.

## Acceptance checks at the receiving desk

Imaging transfer working reference



# Completion record

After acceptance, the coordinator records the receipt date, receiving reviewer, series summary, and any follow-up action. The record should point to the transfer acknowledgement without embedding clinical source details.



### Receipt review

The receiving coordinator confirms that the transfer arrived through the agreed route, opens a representative image in the approved viewer, and compares the listed series against the handoff record. Any discrepancy is returned to the site as a transfer clarification rather than edited at the receiving desk.



## Scope

This reference describes the operational handoff of de-identified renal MRI material for Study Beta, ORCHID-CKD-202. It is used by the local imaging desk and the receiving coordination team after a scheduled imaging visit.

|  Study | Study Beta – ORCHID-CKD-202  |
| --- | --- |
|  Transfer route | Site staging folder to controlled receiving workspace  |
|  Local owner | Imaging desk, Minato Medical Center  |
|  Receiving owner | Asteria Imaging Coordination  |



# Site follow-up

- Keep the local request, transfer record, and acknowledgement together for the site review.
- Route questions about image quality to the imaging lead and questions about visit timing to the site CRC desk.
- Document repeat transfers as a new operational event while preserving the earlier acknowledgement.



### De-identification review

|  Review area | Expected handling  |
| --- | --- |
|  Patient name and identifier | Removed before research transfer  |
|  Institution-specific comments | Reviewed and removed when not required for the study  |
|  Study visit label | Replaced by the approved research identifier  |
|  Acquisition details | Retained only when required for imaging quality review  |



## Before transfer

- Verify that the research identifier is present and that direct identifiers are removed from DICOM headers.
- Compare the series list with the imaging request and document a local receipt reference.
- Keep the clinical source system separate from the research transfer workspace.

This guide supports a controlled operational handoff; clinical interpretation remains local.

# Study Alpha moderator plan

**Study:** Activation and Weekly Plan interviews
**Window:** 2026-06-15 through 2026-07-10
**Session length:** 45 minutes



## Opening

Confirm consent, explain that we are evaluating the product rather than the participant, and ask how grocery planning currently happens in the household. Keep the opener conversational; do not turn it into a survey.



## Core route

1. Invite the participant to begin a first plan in Tidepool.
2. Observe how they choose a familiar meal and whether they look for a weekly overview.
3. Ask them to react to the optional staples panel.
4. Let them edit servings and move from plan to list.
5. Close by asking what they would return to next week.



## Capture

Note direct observations separately from interpretation. Mark prototype build, language used, and any follow-up action. Do not record names or contact information in the session notes.



## Handoff

Send the sidecar check to Research Operations before the end of the next business day. Flag broken links or confusing labels in the findings board, with a screenshot only when consent covers it.

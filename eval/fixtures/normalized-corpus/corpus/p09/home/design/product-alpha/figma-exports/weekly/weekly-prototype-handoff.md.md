# Tidepool weekly prototype handoff

**Week of:** 2026-06-22
**From:** Asha Rao, Product Design
**For:** Study Alpha moderators and note takers



## What changed

- The activation path now asks for one familiar meal before it asks for household preferences.
- A saved-staples panel can seed a weekly plan without implying that the pantry data is complete.
- Quantity editing remains available from both the plan and the shopping list.



## Moderator notes

Start at the welcome frame, then let participants choose their own route. Do not explain the staple labels until they ask. Capture moments where a participant expects a summary before committing to a plan.



## Build notes

Use `alpha-3.4` for sessions after 2026-06-22. The Pantry Shortcuts concept is in a separate flow and should not be introduced during Alpha unless the guide calls for a comparison.

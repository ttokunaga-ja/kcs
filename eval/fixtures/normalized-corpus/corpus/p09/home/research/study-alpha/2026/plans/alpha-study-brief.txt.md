```txt
Tidepool Study Alpha — Activation and Weekly Plan interviews

Purpose
Understand how people begin a useful grocery plan in Tidepool, especially when they have a familiar meal in mind but incomplete pantry information.

Timing
Fieldwork runs from 2026-06-15 through 2026-07-10. Sessions are remote, moderated, and scheduled for 45 minutes.

Participants
Recruit adults who plan groceries for themselves or a household at least once most weeks. Include a mix of people who use notes, retailer apps, and shared household messages.

Questions
- What makes the first plan feel worth saving?
- When do optional staples help, and when do they distract?
- How do people move between a weekly plan and a shopping list?
- What changes when a household schedule shifts midweek?

Deliverable
A short synthesis for Product and Design, with design implications and linked evidence by 2026-07-14.
```

# Study Alpha findings review

**Review date:** 2026-07-03
**Study:** Activation and Weekly Plan interviews



## Working themes

1. Participants looked for a weekly overview before they evaluated the shopping list.
2. Familiar meals gave people an easier starting point than household setup questions.
3. Pantry signals worked when presented as optional cues, not as a claim of complete inventory.
4. People wanted edits to remain visible as their schedule changed.



## Implications for the next build

Keep the plan preview ahead of the list, preserve edit history within the active session, and make starter staples easy to dismiss. The team should test whether a compact explanation of the plan-to-list connection reduces hesitation.



## Evidence handling

These are synthesis candidates, not final product requirements. Link each candidate to a session code before the readout and preserve contrary observations in the board.

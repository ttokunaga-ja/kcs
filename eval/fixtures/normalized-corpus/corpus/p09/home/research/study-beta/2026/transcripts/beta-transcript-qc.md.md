# Study Beta transcript QC



## Review standard

Check the concept conversation against the screen recording notes and the session sidecar. Retain wording that explains expectations, uncertainty, or changes of mind; streamline only clear transcription noise.



## Beta-specific checks

- Confirm whether the participant encountered Pantry Shortcuts without a prompt.
- Mark their expectation before they applied a suggestion.
- Note whether they looked for a review state, meal context, or list context.
- Separate comments about the label from comments about the shortcut behavior.



## Escalation

If consent, recording quality, or screen context is unclear, return the session to Research Operations. Do not infer an action from a single sentence when the interaction history says otherwise.

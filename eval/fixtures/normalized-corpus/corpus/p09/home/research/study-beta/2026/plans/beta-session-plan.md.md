# Study Beta session plan

**Concept:** Tidepool Pantry Shortcuts
**Window:** July 2026
**Session length:** 45 minutes



## Goals

Learn whether people can discover the shortcut, describe what it uses, and decide whether to apply it to a meal plan or a shopping list.



## Tasks

1. Ask the participant to begin a grocery-planning task in the concept prototype.
2. Let them find Pantry Shortcuts without a feature tour.
3. Ask what they think will happen before they select a shortcut.
4. Show the review state and invite them to accept, adjust, or skip suggestions.
5. Compare the result with their usual way of starting a list.



## Notes for analysis

Capture expectations before interaction, not only reactions afterward. Separate a label problem from a concept problem. Mark any moment where the participant asks how a suggestion connects to a planned meal.

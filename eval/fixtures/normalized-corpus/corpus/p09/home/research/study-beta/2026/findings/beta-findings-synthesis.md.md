# Study Beta synthesis — Pantry Shortcuts

**Fieldwork:** July 2026
**Method:** concept validation interviews



## What participants understood

Pantry Shortcuts was read as a quick starting point for a meal or shopping list, not as an automatic inventory system. Participants expected to review any suggested items before they entered a plan.



## What needs refinement

- The entry label should explain that shortcuts use the member's saved staples and recent plans.
- The review step should show which meal or list each suggestion supports.
- A shortcut should be easy to ignore without making the empty state feel like a failure.



## Decision

Continue with a narrow prototype that begins from a saved staple and asks the member to choose a meal context. Compare it with the current manual-start path in the next design review.

Tidepool UXR - Activation & Weekly Plan - Jun-Jul 2026

**Purpose** Use this companion while facilitating and writing field notes. The goal is to understand how people turn a first Tidepool setup into a workable weekly plan.



## Weekly plan and close

Connect the activation moment to routines, timing, and adjustment over a real week.



### Weekly planning prompts

- Walk me through the last time you looked at your weekly plan. What was happening around you?
- How do you decide what belongs in the plan and what stays outside it?
- When a week changes, what do you expect Tidepool to help you do?
- Which reminders or progress cues fit naturally, and which feel easy to ignore?



### Opening prompts

- Think back to the first time you opened Tidepool. What were you hoping to do that day?
- What made the initial setup feel clear, uncertain, or more involved than expected?
- Which decision felt most connected to the week you were trying to organize?
- Was there a moment when you paused, changed direction, or left something for later?

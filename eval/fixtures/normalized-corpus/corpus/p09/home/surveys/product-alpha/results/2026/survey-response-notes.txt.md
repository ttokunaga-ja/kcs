Tidepool follow-up survey notes — July 2026

Thirty-one responses were collected after the Study Alpha fieldwork window. This survey is directional only; it complements the moderated sessions and does not replace them.

Common written themes
- People want to begin with a meal they already know their household will eat.
- A visible weekly plan makes a shopping list easier to review.
- Optional staples are useful when they can be changed quickly.
- Respondents asked for a simple way to reuse a previous plan after a busy week.

Caution
The response pool includes volunteers who already use digital lists. Treat the notes as a prompt for follow-up, not a prevalence estimate.

# Returning shopper journey — Tidepool



## Context

The returning shopper has used Tidepool for a few weeks. They may remember a favorite dinner, but they do not maintain a complete inventory and their household calendar changes often.



## Journey moments

| Moment | Need | Product opportunity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weekend planning | Start with a realistic meal | Surface a familiar dinner and last week's plan |
| Pantry glance | Avoid buying duplicates | Offer optional staples with clear edit controls |
| List review | Know why an item is present | Keep each item linked to its planned meal |
| Midweek change | Adapt without rebuilding | Let the shopper move or skip a meal quickly |



## Design principle

Treat a plan as a draft that belongs to the household. The product should make adjustments visible and reversible rather than insisting on a perfect schedule.

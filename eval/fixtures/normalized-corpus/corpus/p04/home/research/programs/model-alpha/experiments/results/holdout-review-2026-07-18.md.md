The Cedar candidate preserved its advantage on short factual queries while remaining mixed on long, multi-clause requests. Reviewers agreed that the improved ordering is easiest to see when duplicate pages are removed before scoring.

- The robust baseline has fewer surprising inversions when the corpus contains archival pages.

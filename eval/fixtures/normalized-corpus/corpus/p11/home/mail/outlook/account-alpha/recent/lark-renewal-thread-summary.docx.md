## Follow-up plan



### Before the next working session

**Commercial owner.** Maya will consolidate the approved account narrative, remove stale draft language, and confirm that the opportunity record reflects the latest internal owners.

**Solution lead.** The services lead will prepare a short walkthrough of the operating sequence requested by Lark, including where customer teams will need enablement and where internal support is required.

**Revenue Operations.** Revenue Operations will review the decision log and flag any item that requires regional review before the account team treats it as settled.



## Purpose of this record

This summary captures the working position reached in the recent Outlook thread among Lark's procurement lead, the regional operations sponsor, SableSignal services, and the account team. It is a coordination record, not customer-facing offer language. The thread shows broad alignment on the business outcome while leaving a small set of scope and sequencing choices for the next working session.



### Customer-facing language

Use clear language about a phased rollout, shared ownership, and a single place for decisions. Avoid presenting exploratory notes as commitments or comparing Lark's situation to another customer's procurement path. Bellwether Health beta may be discussed internally as a separate learning track, but it is not a pricing or contracting precedent for Lark.



## What the thread settled

Participants agreed that the next discussion should connect the Revenue Cloud rollout to a practical operating rhythm for dispatch and finance teams. The account team will lead with the implementation story, then confirm the commercial path once services and legal have validated the supporting assumptions. Procurement asked for fewer parallel drafts and a clearer record of who can approve a change.

> **THREAD READOUT** Keep one commercial narrative in Revenue Cloud and route any change in scope through the named account owners before it reaches the customer.



### Notes for the account record

The thread reflects a preference for disciplined preparation over a fast exchange of partial answers. The account team should preserve that discipline: retain the customer questions, keep the owner beside every response, and close the loop in the same record used for weekly account review. This will give Lark a consistent experience while helping SableSignal coordinate across sales, services, and legal.

**NEXT CHECKPOINT** Review the consolidated account narrative with the cross-functional team before sending the next external recap.

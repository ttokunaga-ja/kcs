# Bellwether Health — solution outline



## First workflow

Use SableSignal Revenue Cloud as the intake and approval record for a defined group of commercial requests. Each request carries an owner, the relevant approvers, a dated decision history, and a link to the supporting customer context.



## Roles

| Team | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Operations | Own intake rules and queue hygiene |
| Finance | Review pricing or margin-sensitive changes |
| Compliance | Approve the request types that need policy review |
| Field team | Submit complete requests and respond to clarifying questions |



## Delivery approach

Begin with a workshop and a small configuration sprint. Validate the audit view with Amara before inviting a wider seller group. Historical migration is explicitly out of the first phase; the initial success measure is reliable ownership and timely approval on new requests.



## Decision needed

Bellwether needs to nominate one operating owner and provide representative request examples for the workshop.

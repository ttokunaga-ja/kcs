# Lark Logistics — support scope options

**Prepared for:** Priya Nair and Evan Holt
**Purpose:** give the regional admin team a predictable way to resolve Revenue Cloud questions during the renewal period.



## Core coverage

- Monthly operating review with Maya and Customer Success.
- Shared escalation map for quote-exception and policy-routing issues.
- Written recap after each administrator clinic.



## Focused adoption clinic

A short working session for regional administrators using Lark's current routing examples. We would review ownership, handoffs, and the situations that should move directly to the central team. The session is deliberately limited to existing configuration.



## Optional response coverage

A named customer-success contact during the first weeks after the renewal start. This is appropriate if Evan expects a concentrated period of policy updates.



## Proposed next step

Choose the core coverage first; add the clinic only if the regional attendee list supports a useful working session.

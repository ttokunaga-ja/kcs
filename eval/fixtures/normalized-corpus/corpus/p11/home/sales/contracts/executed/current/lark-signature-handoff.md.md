# Lark Logistics — signature handoff checklist



## Before sending

- Legal has completed the comparison of the executed record and the current commercial packet.
- Deal Desk has cleared the base scope and any support attachment.
- Maya has confirmed Lila as procurement coordinator and Priya as executive sponsor.



## Customer handoff

Send one clean packet with the adoption summary, service scope, and signature instructions. Keep the administrator clinic description in its own attachment so the customer can decide whether to include it.



## After receipt

Record the signatory, route the final copy to Customer Success, and schedule the first operating review with the regional admins.

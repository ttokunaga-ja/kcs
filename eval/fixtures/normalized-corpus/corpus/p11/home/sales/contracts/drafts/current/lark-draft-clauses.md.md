# Lark draft clause checklist



## Scope alignment

The draft should describe the deployed Revenue Cloud workflow, including regional exception review and the current administrator model. Do not imply that an optional clinic changes the base service.



## Operational review

- Legal confirms the operative language against the signed packet.
- Deal Desk verifies that the support attachment is separately described.
- Maya checks the customer name, signatory details, and procurement contact before sharing a clean copy.



## Open drafting question

If Lark requests a new carrier-portal feature, handle it as a later scope discussion rather than inserting it into the renewal packet.

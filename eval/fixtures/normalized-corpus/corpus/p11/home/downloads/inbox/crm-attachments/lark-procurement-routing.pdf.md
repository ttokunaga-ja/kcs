# Lark procurement routing

Internal handoff note for the Lark Logistics account | Owner: Maya Chen | Q3 FY2026

**Use this note when** a commercial request needs a coordinated path through procurement, security, legal, or finance. Keep the customer-facing owner singular even when several internal teams contribute.



## Account executive responsibilities

The account executive frames the customer outcome, maintains the chronology, and sends the consolidated update. Specialists should not have to reconstruct the opportunity from scattered messages.



## Before closing the route

Record the customer question in plain language, the team that answered it, the artifact sent, and any follow-up that remains. Link the final customer note to the account timeline rather than leaving the outcome only in chat.

|  Record | Why it matters  |
| --- | --- |
|  Decision summary | Lets the account team preserve intent across absences.  |
|  Shared artifact | Prevents an outdated attachment from re-entering the conversation.  |
|  Open follow-up | Makes ownership visible before the next customer touchpoint.  |

**Final check.** The customer should receive one coherent response, even when the underlying work crossed several SableSignal teams.

3

## Intake checklist

Capture the request in the account workspace with the customer contact, desired business outcome, procurement contact, and any artifact already shared. Confirm whether the item is a standard administrative step or a review that needs specialist input.

|  Route | First action  |
| --- | --- |
|  Procurement | Verify the customer workflow and provide the current commercial packet.  |
|  Security | Collect the use case and route the existing assurance material for review.  |
|  Legal | Identify the requested document and any customer redlines before assigning counsel.  |
|  Finance | Confirm the commercial scenario and obtain the required internal approval.  |

1

## Specialist responsibilities

- State the question being answered and the material needed to answer it.
- Flag dependencies early, especially when another team must review before a response can be shared.
- Return a concise internal recommendation that the account owner can translate for the customer.

**Working rule.** A handoff is complete only when the next owner, expected artifact, and customer update path are explicit.



## Escalation signal

Escalate when the customer asks for a commitment outside the approved commercial package, when the requested security posture differs materially from the documented product use, or when multiple customer teams provide conflicting direction.

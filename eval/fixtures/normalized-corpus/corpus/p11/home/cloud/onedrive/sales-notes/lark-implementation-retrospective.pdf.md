# Lark Logistics implementation retrospective

Internal reflection from the July rollout checkpoint | SableSignal account team

**Summary.** The rollout moved cleanly when regional champions had one place to collect field questions and the delivery desk separated training requests from product feedback.



## What supported progress

- Dispatch leads came to the checkpoint with concrete workflow examples rather than broad status updates.
- The customer nominated visible regional champions, shortening the path from a local question to a useful answer.
- Maya kept customer messaging concise while implementation partners tracked operational detail.



## Follow-up for the account team

Keep the next cohort focused on manager routines, maintain one list of recurring field themes, and bring only decision-ready items into the customer update. The team should preserve the steady cadence that made the handoff feel manageable.

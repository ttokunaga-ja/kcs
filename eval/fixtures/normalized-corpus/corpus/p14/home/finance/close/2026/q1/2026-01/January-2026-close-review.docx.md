# January 2026 Close Review

Finance & Accounting — Orion ERP Working Review

**Period:** January 2026

**Review phase:** Post-close operational review

**Business area:** E-commerce fulfillment and last-mile delivery

**System:** Orion ERP

Purpose and outcome

Review objective

This review records the operational observations from the January 2026 monthly close. It focuses on whether the close team could trace fulfillment support, vendor information, and approval evidence without relying on informal handoffs.

The review is not a replacement for the ledger, the formal close checklist, or retained vendor evidence. Its purpose is to identify small process adjustments that make the next monthly cycle easier to prepare and review.

Overall outcome

The close followed the planned sequence in Orion ERP and the required evidence was available to the reviewer. The main attention point was the timing of delivery confirmations that arrived near the internal review cutoff.

Finance & Accounting and the fulfillment operations team were able to resolve most timing questions before approval. A small set of items was retained for follow-up with named owners and a documented next action.

Scope

The working review covered delivery-cost support, vendor invoice readiness, return-related entries, and the standard period-end journal review for the January cycle.



Actions for the next cycle

February readiness

Open the exception log before the final business day and use consistent labels for delivery timing, incomplete vendor support, and system follow-up. The labels should be practical enough for both the close team and fulfillment operations to use.

Before the approval checkpoint, circulate the active exception list with an owner and next action for every item. Items that remain open need a clear reason and a planned confirmation point.

Evidence and sign-off

Continue to store the approved support in the normal close location and keep the handoff note focused on operating context. This separation makes it easier to find formal evidence while preserving the lessons needed for the next cycle.

Review conclusion

January close procedures are suitable for continued use. The team should confirm during the February review that the exception labels and early vendor outreach are being used consistently across the operating workflow.

## Operational observations

Fulfillment settlement support

Delivery records should be compared using the same service-date basis before they are used as journal support. When an incoming carrier file is late, the exception note needs to identify the source, the current owner, and the next review date.

The January team found that this approach was more useful than a generic completion flag. It gave the reviewer enough context to distinguish a routine timing item from a record that still needed business confirmation.

Vendor invoice follow-up

For Shinonome Transport and Mizuho Logistics, Finance & Accounting should continue early outreach when service summaries are incomplete. The reviewer should see the contact status alongside the proposed accounting treatment.

Kasumigaseki Data Center charges require the service period to be checked against the current agreement before an adjustment is approved. This supports a consistent review even when the invoice description is abbreviated.

Control boundaries

Preparation, review, and approval remained separate for unusual entries. Where operational staff helped clarify a delivery record, the final accounting review was completed by a different member of the close team.

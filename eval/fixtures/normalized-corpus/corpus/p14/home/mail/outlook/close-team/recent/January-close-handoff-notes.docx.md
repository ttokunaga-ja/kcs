# January Close Handoff Notes

Finance & Accounting — Close Team Working Record

**Period:** January 2026

**Business area:** E-commerce fulfillment and last-mile delivery

**System:** Orion ERP

**Purpose:** Close-team handoff before the February working cycle

Handoff overview

Close status

The January close was completed with the normal review sequence across fulfillment revenue support, delivery costs, vendor invoices, and period-end journals. The team used Orion ERP as the common record for preparation and approval.

This note captures the operational context that should travel with the close team. It is intended to make the next review easier to pick up, rather than to replace the formal checklist or approval record.

What worked well

The fulfillment operations team shared its month-end delivery exception list early enough for Finance & Accounting to separate timing items from invoice follow-up. That reduced late clarification requests during the approval window.

The close owner and reviewer used the same evidence location for unusual entries. The handoff was clearer when supporting notes named the business event, the owner, and the planned follow-up date.

Handoff focus

For February, retain the short daily check-in between delivery operations and the close team until all late-arriving carrier records have been categorized. The goal is to keep exception handling visible before journals are submitted for approval.

Items to carry forward

Source-system observations

Orion ERP import status should be reviewed together with the exception reason, not as a count-only check. A completed import can still contain records that need a business owner to confirm the delivery date or service period.

When an operations file is revised, retain the earlier version only as working evidence and point the close reviewer to the latest confirmed list. This keeps the audit trail readable without duplicating the journal rationale.

Vendor follow-up

Shinonome Transport and Mizuho Logistics should remain on the early outreach list when their service summaries arrive after the internal review deadline. The close owner should record the expected response date with the supporting note.

For Kasumigaseki Data Center charges, confirm the service period before posting any adjustment. The February reviewer should compare the recurring charge description with the current contract summary.

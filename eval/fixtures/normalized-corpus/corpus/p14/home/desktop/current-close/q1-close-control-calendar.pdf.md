# Q1 Close Control Calendar

*Reference for the current close desk — FY2026, issued 7 April 2026*

**Purpose** This note records the repeatable controls used for the January–March close of Shinonome Fulfillment. It covers the e-commerce fulfilment, last-mile delivery and warehouse cost streams processed through Orion ERP. It is a desk reference, not a replacement for approval records.



## Exception routing and handoff

**Before the daily review** The coordinator circulates a short exception list: late feeds, missing invoices, disputed route charges, unmatched department codes, and items awaiting a business owner. The list should describe the evidence gap and required decision, not merely the balance movement.

|  Exception | First contact | Expected close-team response  |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  Missing delivery statement | Vendor AP contact | Confirm expected receipt, record a temporary status and schedule a follow-up.  |
|  Unmapped Orion ERP code | Master-data owner | Request the effective date and approved mapping; do not apply an informal substitution.  |
|  Invoice service-period mismatch | Expense owner | Document the service month and obtain the proposed accounting treatment.  |
|  Late operational feed | Warehouse reporting lead | Retain the cutoff note and assess whether the item belongs in the current or next review.  |

**March close handoff** The Q1 file set is complete when all exceptions have a named owner or documented resolution, the review pair has completed its checklist, and the Finance Manager has confirmed the pack release. The next cycle starts with the unresolved-items register, not with a rebuilt worksheet.

**Contacts** Finance and Accounting owns the control calendar. Operations owns shipment and dispatch reporting. Procurement owns supplier master-data changes. Questions should be routed through the close coordinator so the same decision is available to the whole review pair.

Internal working reference

## Review checklist and evidence map

**1. Revenue and fulfilment** Match completed shipments, confirmed returns and merchant-fee summaries. Investigate movements caused by carrier scan delays before posting a late adjustment.

**2. Delivery and warehouse expense** Obtain the current-month statements from Shinonome Transport and Mizuho Logistics. Compare route-level totals with the dispatch summary; retain the statement, the reconciliation sheet, and the reviewer comment together.

**3. Technology and facilities** Check the Kasumigaseki Data Center usage detail against the contract register. Escalate new service identifiers or material changes in allocation keys to the cost-owner before the close approval.

|  Evidence type | Minimum retained information  |
| --- | --- |
|  Orion ERP export | Extract date, legal entity, period, preparer and a reproducible selection path.  |
|  Supplier statement | Vendor name, covered service period, invoice references and exception explanation.  |
|  Operational report | Cutoff time, reporting population, owner and any known missing feed.  |
|  Review note | Question raised, resolution, reviewer, approval date and follow-up owner.  |

**Practical check** When a figure appears plausible but the underlying identifier changed during the month, treat it as an exception. The close team should not infer a mapping from a prior-month file without confirming the change with the process owner.

**Retention** Working copies may remain on the close desk through the review cycle; the approved version belongs in the evidence folder. Avoid attaching duplicate source files to email threads when a stable SharePoint link is available.

Internal working reference

### Standard cadence

|  Timing | Owner | Control point  |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  Last business day | Close coordinator | Freeze the operational extracts and confirm the reporting calendar with warehouse operations.  |
|  Day 1 morning | GL accountant | Compare the Orion ERP journal export with the close trial balance and flag unmapped cost centres.  |
|  Day 1 afternoon | AP lead | Review open supplier invoices, including Shinonome Transport, Mizuho Logistics and Kasumigaseki Data Center.  |
|  Day 2 | Review pair | Reconcile exception notes, supporting files and approver comments before the management pack is drafted.  |
|  Day 3 | Finance manager | Release the reviewed close checklist and retain the sign-off in SharePoint.  |

**Control principle** Each reviewer should be able to trace a balance from the management pack to a dated extract, then to the operational support. Where timing differs between an invoice and its service month, the reviewer records the reason and the next action rather than overwriting the original note.

**Folder convention** Use the month folder for working material and retain final evidence in the Q1 close evidence area. Files received after the review cutoff are logged as late evidence and linked to the next close meeting.

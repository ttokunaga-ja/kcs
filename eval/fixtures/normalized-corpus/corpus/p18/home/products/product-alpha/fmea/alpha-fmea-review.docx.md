# Aster A1 FMEA Review

Pilot change review / July 2026

This review records the cross-functional discussion around the revised housing-edge process. It focuses on how the first-piece check, fixture condition, and evidence retention work together during the pilot period.



## Discussion notes

The workshop did not treat the issue as a single operator action. The review linked tool-change confirmation, fixture inspection, first-piece approval, and the availability of the supporting record.



## Follow-up and ownership

Quality Assurance will collect the first-piece records. Manufacturing Engineering will confirm the revised work instruction at the station. Production Control will schedule the limited lots and retain the lot identifiers.



### Actions agreed

1. Add the fixture-condition field to the work instruction.
2. Require a photographed first-piece record for the pilot lots.
3. Review the retained record before expanding the process change.



### Next checkpoint

The next FMEA checkpoint is held after the documented pilot review. Any broader change requires the normal change-order approval route.

# Working Model QA Note

Project Meridian finance bridge | 18 July 2026



## Working Model QA Note - continued



### Known limitations

Freight surcharge detail remains aggregated in the current source files. Until the logistics breakout arrives, freight should be treated as a directional range.

Eastport mapping is pending client confirmation and is therefore excluded from any supplier-level conclusion.



## Model objective

Translate the May close and purchasing detail into a transparent view of packaging price, volume, freight, and expedite exposure.

The model is designed to support discussion, not to replace the client finance close.



## Checks completed

- Plant totals tie to the imported finance extract.
- Supplier labels follow the approved mapping structure.
- Assumptions are visible and separated from formula-driven calculations.

# Operating model draft



## Proposed daily rhythm

1. Dispatch confirms the route plan at the agreed freeze time.
2. Maintenance posts vehicle substitutions in the shared exception queue.
3. The depot supervisor owns any route change after freeze and records the customer impact.
4. A short end-of-shift review captures repeatable causes for the next day's plan.



## Design principles

- One visible owner for each route exception.
- A small, repeatable set of reason codes.
- Escalation only when the customer promise or safety threshold is affected.

This draft is intentionally operational. It should be tested with two depots before it is presented as a network-wide standard.

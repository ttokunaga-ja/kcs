# Procurement interview - controller perspective

**Session:** 17 July 2026 | **Attendees:** Plant controller, procurement lead, Harbor & Field Advisory



## Observations

The controller described a practical split between contracted packaging spend and short-notice spot buys. Spot buys are coded consistently at the central plant but are mixed into maintenance accounts at Eastport. That treatment makes the existing monthly variance report understate expedite exposure.



## Themes to validate

- The supplier scorecard is reviewed monthly, but plant teams use different service-failure thresholds.
- Purchase-order approvals above the local threshold are visible in the ERP extract; amendments after receipt are not.
- Finance can provide a three-month view of expected bottle and carton demand by plant.

Confirm the Eastport account mapping with the regional analyst and bring the final crosswalk to the 24 July finance review.

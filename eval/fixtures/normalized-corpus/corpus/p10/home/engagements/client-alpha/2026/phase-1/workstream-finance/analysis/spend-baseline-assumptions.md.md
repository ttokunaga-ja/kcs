# Spend baseline assumptions



## Purpose

This note records the assumptions used to translate the May close and purchasing extracts into the Project Meridian spend baseline. It is a working calculation note, not a client decision memo.



## Assumptions

| Driver | Current treatment | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Packaging demand | May run rate adjusted for confirmed July production plan | Avoids using promotional volume as the steady-state base |
| Freight | Kept separate from supplier unit-price movement | Allows commercial and network actions to be discussed independently |
| Spot buys | Tagged when receipt date falls outside the contracted delivery window | Highlights resiliency cost without reclassifying source transactions |
| Inventory | Valued at recorded receipt cost | Matches the finance close and preserves audit trail |



## Controls

- Each plant total must reconcile to the finance extract before the bridge is circulated.
- Assumption changes are logged in the working model before the next Steering Committee pre-read.
- The scenario range is directional until the supplier timing review closes.

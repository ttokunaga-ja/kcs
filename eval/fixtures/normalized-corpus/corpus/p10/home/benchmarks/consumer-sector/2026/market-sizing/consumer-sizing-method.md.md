# Consumer packaging market-sizing method



## Scope

This quick method is used for early client conversations where the team needs a transparent directional view before commissioning proprietary research.



## Approach

1. Start with addressable households or outlets from a named public or client source.
2. Apply an explicit usage frequency and average unit assumption.
3. Separate structural demand drivers from channel-shift effects.
4. Record each assumption and its owner in the working model.



## Review questions

- Does the unit definition match the client's commercial planning convention?
- Are annual volumes consistent with known production capacity?
- Which assumptions can be replaced with client data during diligence?

The output is a planning range, not a market forecast.

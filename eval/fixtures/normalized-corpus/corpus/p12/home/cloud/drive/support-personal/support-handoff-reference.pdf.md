# Support Handoff Reference

A practical guide for leaving the next owner with enough context to act.



## Prepare the handoff before the shift ends

The goal is not to rewrite the entire case history. Leave the next owner a short, current picture: what the customer sees, what has already been checked, and what should happen next.

1. Open the current case record and remove stale assumptions from the working note.
2. State the customer-facing condition in plain language, without copying internal diagnosis into the reply plan.
3. List completed checks with their outcome. A concise negative result is still useful when it rules out repeat work.
4. Name the next decision or observation that would change the plan.

A useful handoff answers three questions

What is happening now? What is already known? What should the next owner do first? If one of these is missing, add it before moving on.



## Suggested case note structure

CURRENT STATE

Describe the visible behavior and whether the customer can continue with a temporary path.

CHECKS COMPLETED

Record the checks in the order performed, with the outcome of each.

NEXT OWNER

State the first action, the expected evidence, and the condition that should trigger an update.



## Before you step away

1. Confirm that the incoming owner has access to the case record and the referenced materials.
2. Leave one clear next action rather than a broad request to investigate.
3. If customer contact is pending, indicate the message purpose and the information needed before sending it.
4. Mark work as waiting only when the dependency and the expected signal are both recorded.



## Quality checks before assigning the next owner

- The wording separates confirmed observations from hypotheses.
- Any customer promise is visible and has a clear follow-up path.
- Links point to durable case materials, not temporary personal workspaces.
- Attachments are described by purpose so the recipient knows why each matters.
- The owner can begin without asking who last touched the case.

Avoid the two common traps

Do not paste an unfiltered conversation transcript, and do not leave a one-line note that only says the work is still in progress. Both force the next owner to reconstruct context under time pressure.

A readable handoff is a service to both the customer and the next responder.

## A quick read-back

Ask the incoming owner to read back the current state and first action in their own words. This brief check catches missing context more reliably than adding more background to the note.

|  **If the case is...** | Leave this behind  |
| --- | --- |
|  **Waiting on the customer** | The exact question and the reason it is needed.  |
|  **Waiting on another team** | The dependency, current contact point, and what unblocks the case.  |
|  **Ready for a reply** | A short reply plan tied to the latest verified information.  |
|  **Ready to close** | The outcome, any customer confirmation, and reusable learning for future cases.  |

Final reminder

A handoff should reduce uncertainty, not transfer it. Keep it current, specific, and safe to share within the support workflow.

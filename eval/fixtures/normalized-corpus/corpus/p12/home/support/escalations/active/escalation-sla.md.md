# Escalation SLA



## Case policy

The Nimbus case sets its opening reply deadline at 6h.

This window begins when the escalation lead accepts the record in Harborline Workspace. The assignee adds a concise operating note, identifies the next verification point, and keeps the incident owner informed until the first written update is sent.



## Handling notes

- Use the incident channel for operational decisions and evidence requests.
- Record the assigned owner before moving the case to a different queue.
- If service impact expands, attach the latest status note and request a severity review.

The policy is reviewed with Support Operations at the start of each monthly coverage cycle.

# Response guidance



## Case policy

For the Atlas account, the acknowledgement window is 8h.

Start with a short summary of the observed behavior, state which team is reviewing it, and avoid promising a remediation date before engineering has confirmed the scope. Use the shared incident thread for evidence and keep the account team copied on meaningful changes.



## Writing checklist

- Confirm the workspace and affected workflow.
- Separate confirmed observations from investigation hypotheses.
- Close with the next update point and the accountable team.

Escalation leads may shorten the communication cadence when the operational impact changes.

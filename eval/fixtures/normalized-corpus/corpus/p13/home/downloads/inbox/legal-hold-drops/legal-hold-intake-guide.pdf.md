# Handover to reviewers

Provide reviewers with the inventory and receipt record, not just a folder link. This helps the team distinguish a source package from a later working copy.

Update the intake register after each handover so that the current holder and the next action are visible to Legal Operations.

# Preparing for review

Create an inventory that identifies the data type and the source system. Check that the recipient list for the review workspace is limited to the assigned matter team.

Flag missing owners, unreadable files, and likely duplicates for confirmation. Do not remove a duplicate candidate before the review owner has recorded a decision.

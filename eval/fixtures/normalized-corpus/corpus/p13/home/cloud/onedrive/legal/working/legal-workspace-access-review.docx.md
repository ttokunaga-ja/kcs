# Privilege Log Reconciliation

OneDrive legal workspace access review



## Method and observations

We compared the workspace membership list with the legal operations access register and checked recent download activity for privileged draft folders. The review also sampled shared links created for contract negotiation and data-room preparation.

The sampled records include an owner, a business purpose, and a current access level. Items without an active owner were routed to the workspace administrator for follow-up.

## Next actions

Legal Operations will repeat the link audit after the next contract template refresh and will retain this paper with the access register. New matter workspaces should begin with read-only defaults until a reviewer is assigned.

This review is an internal working record and does not replace a matter-specific instruction from outside counsel.

# Legal Hold Acknowledgement Status

Delivery and receipt review for active custodians



## Follow-up approach

The next review will focus on overdue acknowledgements and any newly identified systems. The legal operations team will keep a concise audit trail of reminders and responses.

This document is an operational status paper and should be read together with the current matter instructions.

3

## Operational observations

Where a recipient has changed roles, Legal Operations confirms the current manager and identifies the replacement custodian before updating the distribution record. Questions about ordinary business workflows are answered through the legal operations help channel.

Receipt records are kept with the matter intake register so that delivery evidence is separate from substantive legal analysis.

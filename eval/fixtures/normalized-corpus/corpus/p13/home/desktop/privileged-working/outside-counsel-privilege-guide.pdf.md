# Outside Counsel Privilege Guide

TracePort | Internal legal operations reference



# Practical handling

Before sharing a draft, confirm the recipient list and whether the document contains internal comments. Links should be created with the narrowest access that supports the review task.

When a business update and a legal-review question are both needed, send separate messages or clearly separate the two topics in the record.



# Record maintenance

Keep an access record for restricted folders and remove expired links promptly. If a recipient changes roles, confirm the successor before moving any working materials.

Questions about classification should be routed to Legal Operations before a document is uploaded to a shared project site.



Outside Counsel Privilege Guide
TracePort | Internal legal operations reference
Purpose of this guide
This guide describes how Legal Operations keeps communications with outside counsel separate from ordinary
project coordination. The aim is to preserve a reliable working record while allowing the business team to
continue routine work.
Use the restricted matter workspace for legal-review drafts, advice requests, and counsel responses. Keep the
original message headers when an email is exported for review.
Working control
The assigned owner records the action taken, the location of the working copy, and any access change that
affects the review. This keeps operational handling distinct from the underlying legal analysis.
Recordkeeping
Keep a concise note of follow-up items with the relevant workspace record. If a source is replaced or re-
exported, preserve the earlier receipt record and explain the reason for the later copy.

## Uses that need no permission

- Stating truthfully that your work uses, supports, integrates with, extends,
  or is based on Kio. For example: "Works with Kio", "a Kio plugin",
  "imports Kio archives", "forked from Kio".
- Referring to Kio in articles, talks, documentation, comparisons, reviews, and
  academic work, including critical ones.
- Keeping the name intact in unmodified copies you redistribute — in fact the
  license's [Notices](LICENSE.md#notices) section requires you to pass the
  required-notice lines along.
- Using the name in file paths, package metadata, or configuration where it
  merely identifies the upstream project you are interoperating with.

The common thread: describing a relationship to Kio is fine. Presenting your
thing *as* Kio is not.

## Uses that need permission

- Naming your product, service, company, or a distributed fork "Kio", or a
  name confusingly similar to it.
- Publishing a package, app, or hosted service under the name "Kio" in any
  registry or store.
- Registering a domain name whose distinctive part is "Kio" in a way that
  suggests it is the official project.
- Using the name or any Kio logo in a way that implies endorsement,
  affiliation, certification, or official status.
- Modifying the Kio name or logo and continuing to present it as Kio.

**If you distribute a modified version, rename it.** Keep an accurate
attribution — "based on Kio" — but ship it under your own name. This is the
single most important rule here: it lets users tell whose bug they are hitting.

## Enforcement

The purpose of this policy is to keep the name a reliable signal of origin, not
to police discussion of the project. We do not intend to object to accurate,
non-confusing references, and we will not use trademark claims to suppress
criticism, comparison, or interoperability.

If you believe a use is fine but this document reads as prohibiting it, open an
issue — the wording is probably too broad and we would rather fix it.

- 「Kio に対応」「Kio のプラグイン」「Kio をフォーク」など、Kio との関係を**事実として**述べること

- 無改変の再頒布において名称を保持すること (ライセンスの [Notices](LICENSE.md#notices) 節は

要点は「Kio との**関係を説明する**のは自由、自分の成果物を Kio **そのものとして提示する**のは不可」です。

- 製品名・サービス名・社名・頒布するフォークの名称を「Kio」または混同を招く類似名にすること

**改変版を頒布する場合は改名してください。** 「based on Kio」といった正確な帰属表示は残しつつ、
ご自身の名前で公開してください。利用者が「どちらのバグか」を判別できることが、この規則の目的です。

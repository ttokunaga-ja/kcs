## What the default license allows

PolyForm Shield 1.0.0 permits **any purpose** — including commercial and
internal business use — **except** providing a product that competes with Kio,
or that competes with any product the licensor provides using Kio.

Read [Noncompete](LICENSE.md#noncompete) and [Competition](LICENSE.md#competition) in
the license for the operative wording. Note in particular that the license
treats goods and services as competing even when they are free of charge, and
even when they expose their functionality through a different kind of interface.

### You almost certainly do **not** need a commercial license to

- Run Kio on your own machines, personally or across a company of any size.
- Index your organization's own documents and search them internally.
- Use Kio in research, teaching, or evaluation.
- Modify Kio for your own use, including private forks.
- Build and ship a product that uses Kio internally but is not itself a
  substitute for Kio.
- Contribute to Kio. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

### You likely **do** need a commercial license to

- Offer Kio, or a fork of it, as a hosted or managed service.
- Ship a product whose value proposition substantially overlaps Kio's —
  local knowledge archiving, evidence-grounded local search, or local file
  indexing marketed as an alternative to Kio.
- Embed Kio into a product you market as a practical substitute for Kio.
- Redistribute Kio as a component of any of the above.

These lists are illustrative. They do not enlarge or narrow the license.

## Why this license

Kio is developed in the open so that its behavior can be audited — an archive
that claims your evidence will not die is worth little if you cannot verify the
claim. PolyForm Shield keeps the source readable, forkable, and usable for
essentially every ordinary purpose, while reserving the single case of someone
reselling Kio as their own competing product.

If a use you consider legitimate appears to be blocked, please open an issue.
The boundary is meant to catch resale, not adoption.
